# Shopify themes

Free Shopify themes, ready to upload to your store. Each theme lives in its own folder.

| Theme | Folder | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Barnyard** | [`barnyard`](barnyard) | Pet treat and pet food stores, with bold colours and scroll animations |
| **Storefront** | [`theme`](theme) | General online stores |

## How to install a theme

### 1. Download the files

1. At the top of this page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip the file on your computer.

### 2. Make the theme zip

1. Open the folder of the theme you want, for example `barnyard`.
2. Select **everything inside** that folder (`assets`, `config`, `layout`, `locales`, `sections`, `snippets`, `templates` and the other files).
3. Compress those files into one zip.
   - Windows: right-click the selected files > **Send to** > **Compressed (zipped) folder**.
   - Mac: right-click the selected files > **Compress**.

Zip the files inside the theme folder, not the folder itself. If you zip the folder, Shopify will not recognise the theme.

### 3. Upload to Shopify

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Click **Add theme > Upload zip file** and choose your zip.
3. Wait for the upload to finish. The theme appears under your theme library.

### 4. Make it yours

1. Click **Customize** on the new theme.
2. Change your logo and colours in **Theme settings**.
3. Click each section to change its text and images.
4. Click **Save**.

### 5. Publish

When everything looks right, click **Publish**. Your old theme stays in the library, so you can switch back at any time.

## Barnyard setup tips

- **Protein colours:** tag products `protein-chicken`, `protein-turkey`, `protein-beef` or `protein-fish` to give their tiles and pages that colour. Products without these tags use a neutral colour.
- **Best sellers:** create a collection, for example "Best sellers", and choose it in the home page **Best sellers** section.
- **Toys and accessories:** on these products, set **Theme template** to `accessory` for a simpler product page.
- **Sample content:** text marked "Sample" and the demo pictures are placeholders. Replace them with your own.

## Good to know

- These themes are free to use on your own store.
- They are custom themes, so Shopify Support does not help with them. Reach out to me for help.
- Your products, prices and orders stay in your Shopify store. A theme only changes how your store looks.
